# 渡口漫画概念图的生成指令

使用内置 ImageGen。图稿在同一斜俯视横屏构图下，依次修改人物与城门的比例、画面的明暗和人物造型。

## 第 1 稿

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: an actual in-game SCREEN composition concept for an original stylized nonpixel mobile strategy game, "Evolution War: River Ford" (do not write the title). Ultra-wide horizontal 2:1 aspect ratio, phone landscape, screenshot area only, no phone mockup. Strong original 2D hand-painted graphic novel and dark, adventurous folktale aesthetic. Art directed as a cohesive animated comic, thin internal pencil strokes and strong ink silhouettes, big flat two-step shadows and color planes, subtle dry gouache at riverbanks only. No physically rendered surfaces, no clay toy figures, no realistic anatomy, no pixel art, no existing characters or logos.
Camera and geometry: high fixed orthographic oblique view about 50 degrees looking downward; no sky or horizon, left-to-right action. Entire battlefield visible, a broad horizontal river ford occupies 52% of full frame height (road upper edge around y24%, lower edge around y76%), wavering banks with bluish river visible above and below that crossing. The shallow, warm light gray-sand crossing goes edge to edge; keep its center nearly flat and clean. Both gates are at the extreme screen edges, left one center x=6% and right one x=94%, both fully visible above HUD, small enough that they don't consume the runway. Each is an actual low defensive stockade gate with a clearly readable inward-facing dark opening, short wall and banner. Left side has one visible upgrade in the form of a low stone wing attached to the wooden gateway. A tiny split monolith at upper left, a small crooked orange tree and dark stylized sedges along the outer edges. Mid-distance player camera, no poster focus.
Characters and action: 16 small readable 3-to-3.5-head-tall human folk-warriors, each body and helmet SMALL about 8% of total image height, no big hero. Team indigo-teal with angular felt helmets and broad rectangular shields, opponent brick red with notched russet pennants and light round shields. Distinct silhouettes from small size: shield infantry braced low and wide, javelin troops slender with exaggerated arm anticipation and dark tipped long spears. At center, two or three blue shields stand side by side across the road depth, with a staggered blue throwing line behind; red vanguard closes up and a few red reinforcements emerge from the far right gate. Leave two sizable empty spaces between clash and gates. Unit front/back dependence clear from foot points and discrete short contact patches. A SINGLE compact ivory shield-impact slash at the contact, minimal effects. Readable at phone scale.
Visual direction: cold navy-blue and pine green riverbanks, deep blue-green water with sparse graphic curves, muted warm gray sand with just enough warm light to peek the contrast of troops. Saturated bits only on troops, tiny gate lanterns and flags. Deliberately graphic, richly tasteful, adult adventurous animation with chibi proportions, some irregular handcrafted edges but distinct flat cel shadows. Stage pose, costume, timber, stones, and trees all drawn with a shared dark ink line and graphic shapes; no gradients except subdued atmospheric bank falloff, no glowing fog over combat, no pebbles littering the road.
Actual UX reserved: a tiny muted cyan health bar in the top left and a tiny red health bar in the top right, well away from bases. Very small bottom centered horizontal command strip with three inked flat icons (rectangular shield, javelin, wall), nothing else. All UI is graphical ONLY, strictly no words, no numerals, no logos. Command strip must not be tall or fill full width, must not cover fight. A beautiful coherent graphic-novel game still, high readability, low clutter.
```

## 第 2 稿

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Image 1 is the edit target, an original 2:1 phone-landscape hand-painted strategy game mock screenshot. Make one coherent change: correct the playable scale and breathing room. Keep the exact 2:1 composition, river, crooked autumn tree, split stone, warm sandbar, original flat inked cartoon style and palette, dark forest framing, top bars, bottom 3-icon dock, and lighting. Keep the roadway spanning the full width and its top and bottom bounds exactly in place. Keep the castle gates at the extreme left and right, each center x about 6% or 94%; scale the entire visible gate structure down about 18%, reveal the clipped bits of the walls by pulling them into the safe border while keeping them edge-anchored and keeping each dark doorway visible. 
Crucial: redraw each soldier at about 72–75% of their current height and width in image space (fighters currently too large). Maximum full-body figure including helmet is 9% of total image height, about 78 pixels in a 887 px example. Small black grounded shape at each new foot. Shift the two factions' main groups about 3% toward the center, preserve the front line touching and readable, spread the shield group across 3 local depth positions so they look side by side in perspective and don't form one vertical stack of merged shields. Keep the scattered rear throwers and the two or three reinforcements at the right gate. Keep a big visible sand gap between the reinforcements and the center clash on both sides. Compact ivory shield contact flash is a little smaller with the new scale. Keep each troop's identity and silhouette: angular blue hats with square chevron shields, rust red brimmed hats with round cream shields, long javelins behind. Do not make enlarged faces or oversized framing; the whole play area is the subject. Retain hand-inked lines, flat 2D costume fills and surface, soft ambient colors. No new elements, no sky, no titles or text, no 3D, no photo realism, no wider or narrower overall crop, no change to HUD positions.
```

## 第 3 稿

```text
Use case: style-transfer via a targeted edit. Image 1 is the only edit target, OUR original 2:1 horizontal playable battlefield at an open river ford. Image 2 is an abstract mood reference for the visual principles ONLY: selective near-black shadow masses beside vivid colored light, expressive weird-cute shape exaggeration, and hand-inked flat rendering. Use zero characters, architecture, faces, symbols, altar, stained glass or composition from Image 2. Preserve original independent design and the outside river setting. 
Make one overarching change to Image 1: push it convincingly from muted historical graphic novel toward playful, uncanny dark-folktale animation. Preserve EXACT camera, aspect ratio, sideways wide path geometry and very small soldier scale, same number and near-exact foot positions of units, left and right gates at extreme edges, split monolith and bent autumn tree, top health bars and bottom three simple controls, no printed lettering. Do not zoom at all, keep the road wide with abundant clear space. Blue angular hats + blue outlined chevron rectangular shields vs red hats + circular sun shields remain the identifying anchors. Original human storybook silhouettes, slightly more theatrical and expressive at this SAME small size: clean squash in body shapes, huge cheek/helmet silhouette small face, brows in stronger poses, stronger useful variation between wide hunched shield units and linear longer-armed throwers. No animals, lambs, occult insignia, glowing eyes, crowns, church, or other characters from reference. Scale unchanged.
Art and light: a dramatic twilight storybook with large almost-black deep violet-blue masses in the banks and corners, hand-drawn warped near-black plant and roof silhouettes, clean graphic colored light. Restrained but much more SATURATED ACCENT COLORS than Image 1: cyan-teal moonlight catching the upper bank and some shield edges, clear orange-red lantern pools right at low gates, a little muted violet in river shadows, and warm ivory/copper reflections. It is important to get a theatrical chiaroscuro effect through the juxtaposition of dark masses and bright accent colors rather than by darkening the whole frame. Middle of road gently amber-lit muted beige, large bright and clean enough to unambiguously read the troops from the size of a phone. Keep accent pools local at edges so path positions and gaps are obvious; no colored beams across play area. Give both original gate roofs a subtle hand-drawn warped geometry consistent with the troops and blades of grass, simple tarred wood black silhouette with warm edge. Use single clear ink-line family across environment, figures and GUI icons: bold organic outside stroke, pared-down interiors and chunky flat hand painted colors; remove obvious uniform paper noise across the road. Compact warm ivory contact mark, no visual noise. Preserve the cliff-free top-down-oblique fixed gameplay framing. The result should read as distinctly spooky-whimsical, with character, vivid color and big graphic shapes, in a consistent expressive 2D animation world. Original concept.
```
