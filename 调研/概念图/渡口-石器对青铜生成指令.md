# 渡口石器对青铜生成指令

两稿使用内置 ImageGen。第二稿沿用第一稿的两时代设计，另用手机构图参考校正人物与城门比例。

## 第 1 稿

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. One original game-art screenshot concept for a 2:1 landscape mobile army strategy game. One wide battlefield, ASYMMETRIC TWO DIFFERENT CIVILIZATION AGES at once. LEFT STONE-AGE clan defending a river ford, RIGHT BRONZE-AGE city army trying to take it. Make comparison obvious on a small phone from architecture, weapon shapes, costume shapes and group behavior even in grayscale. One hand-drawn dark-folktale animated comic throughout, no split-screen no panels, no logos, no text, no HUD or lettering. One fictional world, no identifiable character or asset from any existing game. 
CAMERA: fixed orthographic steep oblique looking down 50 degrees, horizontal left-right gameplay, no horizon, no sky. Two playable entrance doors are at outer edges, center positions x=6% and x=94%, opposite ends on the SAME ground depth, BOTH open passages fully visible, keep a tiny safe edge margin. A rich river curves vertically behind and in front of a very broad sandy shoal route running horizontally from x=0 to x=100%; route spans from y=24% to y=75%. People can visibly stand shoulder to shoulder at 4 or more different ground depths. High readable game view, not poster composition. All full-body soldiers SMALL around 7 to 8% of image height each, ample clear space between two doors and center skirmish. Paths and ground shadows show coherent depth. Sixteen to eighteen troops total. 
LEFT edge building: very low asymmetric rough settlement entrance made of TWO chunky split natural gray boulders and a heavy crooked raw tree trunk bridging across them, a single little hide banner stretched on side posts, low amber brazier and a couple stacked unworked stones at the base. Strong cave-mouth opening shape. Absolutely no stone-brick masonry, no roof-eaves, no towers, no metal, no heraldic fabric pennant. LEFT fighters: about eight unruly early stone people crouch and fan loosely across the central-left shallows, two sturdy rounded hide-tunic shield carriers with oversized irregular hide shields or stone clubs, two elastic skinny sling throwers with a V shaped strap and one raised arm, bone clasps in hair, bare knees, messy angular dark hair, thick shoulders and large gesture. Simple strong identities from a distance, material colors bone ivory, clay ash and warm tawny leather; a small saffron knot on each. Keep their humans individual and ingenious, no helmets and no matching uniforms. 
RIGHT edge building: low broad angular ashlar bronze-age city gate, large cut-stone blocks, square bronze-trimmed doorway in a rectangular wall, a compact square parapet and NOTCHED woven flag with an original geometric chevron. Warm dull amber edge and verdigris accents on metal. Clearly BUILDING exposure at the extreme screen edge. RIGHT fighters: narrow controlled ranks of bronze armor with tall distinct helmets, green-patina chest plates and angular long shields with copper edging, longer lean arms and narrow heads, spears projecting in a tight formation. Two waves: a compact skewed formation presses from the center-right, with two separate fresh figures emerging through the right gate farther back. No mirror poses. No cartoon wizard hats. 
ACTION: two different formation shapes just about to coalesce on one road. A few left warriors are braced in an irregular crescent and a stone slinger is taking position around the flank; the right shield formation has 3 legible overlapping fronts and two visible spears behind. No line of staged 1v1 fights, no identical mirrored rows. Point of contact has one little ivory spark as stone hits bronze. Separation between backs and fronts is legible.
COLOR AND STYLE: genuinely flat ink-and-gouache 2D cartoon, long loose black lines, wet cut-paper silhouettes, hard shadow shapes, a bit of rough hand-painted texture only at edges. Quirky, folklore creature-like human proportions, naturally warm expressive compact eyes, no baby heads, no smooth 3D glossy toys or naturalistic adult anatomy. Scene is balmy night with large almost-black aubergine shapes, desaturated turquoise blue water, quietly lit broad gray-ivory sandbar for the readable action. Selective accents from left amber fire and right smoky copper-green lantern. Bunched warped dark reeds and a crooked black tree at the very top outer corners. Less texture and lower contrast at the center to keep shapes readable. Original expressive dark folktale, art-directed asymmetry and information clarity, at actual game zoom.
```

No 3D, no realism, no words, labels, icons, close-up, blood, watermarks or logos.
